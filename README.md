# Health360: Comprehensive Healthcare Risk & Disease Analysis

## Project Overview
This data analytics project analyzes a dataset of 5,000 patients to identify hidden patterns, risk factors, and high-risk patient groups for chronic diseases such as Heart Disease, Diabetes, and Stroke. The goal is to provide actionable insights for healthcare professionals, data analysts, and public health researchers.

## Problem Statement
Delayed detection of health risk factors leads to severe medical complications. This project addresses the challenge of identifying high-risk patients early by analyzing demographic details (Age, Gender), clinical indicators (BMI, Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, Glucose), and lifestyle behaviors (Smoking, Alcohol, Exercise). 

## Tools Used
*   **Tableau Public:** Used for data preparation, creating interactive visualizations, building a comprehensive dashboard, and presenting a data story.
*   **Dataset:** `smart_healthcare_dataset.csv` (5,000 synthetic patient records, 16 variables).

## Key Visualizations & Findings
1.  **Clinical Risk Assessment:** Analyzed the relationship between specific clinical indicators and diseases (e.g., Average Cholesterol by Heart Disease, Average Glucose by Diabetes) to identify baseline risk profiles.
2.  **Lifestyle Behavior Impact:** Created stacked bar charts to uncover correlations between lifestyle habits, such as smoking, and the prevalence of Heart Disease.
3.  **Public Health Demographics:** Utilized data binning to group patients by age, revealing that the risk and occurrence of Heart Disease increase significantly in patient groups over the age of 50.
